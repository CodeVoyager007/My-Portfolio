# ServeEase

ServeEase is a high-end, all-in-one hospitality management platform designed to digitize the fine dining experience. It bridges the gap between guest experience and back-of-house operations, providing a seamless digital standard for elite restaurants.

## Key Features

- **Digital Guest Experience**: A bespoke, browser-based digital menu that requires no app downloads, focusing on elegant UI and instant load times.
- **Intelligent Kitchens**: A real-time Kitchen Display System (KDS) to synchronize dining room orders with kitchen management.
- **Merchant & Owner Dashboards**: Comprehensive analytics providing predictive ROI and operational insights.
- **Global Scalability**: Built-in support for multi-location management and secure, private payment processing.

## Technologies Used

- **Next.js**: For a fast and responsive frontend.
- **Tailwind CSS**: For modern and responsive styling.
- **Vercel**: For high-performance deployment and hosting.
