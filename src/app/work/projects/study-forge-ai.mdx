# StudyForge AI

Study Forge AI is a web application that leverages artificial intelligence to provide 18 essential study tools for students. It helps users learn more efficiently by generating flashcards, summarizing long texts, and creating practice quizzes tailored to their specific topics.

## Key Features

- **Flashcard Generation**: Automatically creates flashcards from any text or topic.
- **Text Summarization**: Condenses long articles or notes into concise summaries.
- **Practice Quizzes**: Generates MCQs to test your knowledge.
- **Topic-Based Learning**: Enter a topic and get a comprehensive study guide.

## Technologies Used

- **React & TypeScript**: For a robust and type-safe frontend.
- **Supabase**: For backend services and database management.
- **AI Integration**: Powered by advanced language models for content generation.
- **Tailwind CSS**: For a clean and modern user interface.

## Goal

The goal of StudyForge AI is to make studying smarter, not harder, by automating the creation of study materials and allowing students to focus on understanding concepts.
